01

Curatorial judgment

Which work belongs

Vietnam's art world is among the most vital in Southeast Asia, and among the hardest to read with clarity. We move through it from the inside. The question we answer is never simply which work is significant, but which work belongs.

02

A community of artistry

Bonds across generations

Our practice rests on enduring relationships across Vietnam's art world. Some of these bonds span generations. What is created together sustains the scene itself.

03

Scholarship before making

Study has the first word

Every commission begins in study: the history of a place, the architecture and light the work will live within. This grounding is what separates a collection from an acquisition.

04

Museum standards of care

Made to museum standard

Every work leaves HOATAY prepared to endure, made with the materials and methods of museum practice for spaces that are lived in every day.

05

Built to last

A lasting asset

Every piece is made to endure and simple to care for, with materials chosen for the daily life of a working hotel. Delivered complete, the collection carries the story of the property, gives guests and press something to remember, and keeps working for the hotel year after year.
