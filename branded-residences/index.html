How we work

From first brief to final handover

The same process behind our hotel commissions, with clear documents at every stage for your design, procurement and sales teams.

01

Brief & site study

We walk the site, read the drawings, and listen to the design team and the operator. From this we shape an art strategy for the property: its narrative, its zones, its budget and its timeline.

Why it mattersOne direction for design, operations and budget.
02

Concept & curation

A concept presentation for every area, with artists, mediums and placements, refined with you until it is approved.

Why it mattersA clear vision to review, refine and approve.
03

BOQ & specification

Every piece is itemised in a bill of quantities and documented in a specification book, with dimensions, materials, framing and fixing, ready for budgeting or tender.

Why it mattersA sound basis for pricing, tender and procurement.
04

Production & quality control

Works are made by our artists, partner workshops and our own workshop. Our team inspects each piece at the workshop before it leaves for site.

Why it mattersEvery piece arrives on site as approved.
05

Installation & handover

We install and sign off each area with your team, then hand over a complete record of the collection: a schedule of every piece as installed, with its location, materials and dimensions.

Why it mattersA clear record for the team who will care for the collection.
